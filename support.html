Common questions

Do I need an account to use Tonight?
No — the free version of Tonight works without creating an account.

What's included in Premium?
Premium unlocks personalized recommendations built from your watchlist and viewing history, plus more features on the way. Everything else — episode tracking, tonight's new episodes, new releases, trending, and streaming availability — is free.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Premium subscriptions are managed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store — you can cancel anytime from your app store account settings.

Where does Tonight get its show and streaming data?
Show information comes from TMDB, and streaming availability is sourced via JustWatch.
