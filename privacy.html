What this covers

This policy describes what information Tonight collects when you use the app, and how it's used. Tonight is built to keep as much as possible on your own device.

Information stored on your device

Your watchlist, episode tracking, and streaming service preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not collected by us and is not visible to us — it stays on your device unless you choose to export it yourself.

Third-party services we use

Tonight uses The Movie Database (TMDB) to retrieve show information, artwork, and episode data, and streaming availability data sourced via JustWatch. When you use Tonight, requests are made to these services to display this information. Tonight is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't require an account to use the free features of Tonight.

Accounts & subscriptions

If you create an account or subscribe to Premium, we collect the minimum information needed to manage your subscription (such as your email address and subscription status). Payment is handled by the app store (Google Play or the App Store) or our payment processor — we do not store your payment card details.

Children's privacy

Tonight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Tonight after a change means you accept the updated policy.
